Gotchas learned (now in rms/README.md + ingest-rack-components.sh):
- Image lives in components-dev mirror; needs the carbide-dev/components-dev pull secret.
- [nvlink_config] requires allow_insecure (else nico-api crashloops).
- rack materializes only when a component (switch/shelf) is CREATED with a rack_id — the
  already-ingested hosts don't trigger it; ensure_rack_exists fires from switch/shelf creation.
- Declaring != materializing: must refresh BMCs iteratively.
